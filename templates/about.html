Why we built this

Finding and treating dental caries early is key to good oral health, but diagnosis usually depends on a visual examination at a dental clinic, which is not always convenient or accessible. This project offers a quick, accessible first check: a computer vision system that looks for signs of caries in photos people take at home, so they can make an informed decision about seeking professional care.

How it works

Each photo is analysed by two object detection models (YOLOv8x and YOLOv8m) trained on different dental datasets. Before inference, contrast can be evened out with CLAHE to reduce the effect of uneven lighting. The two models' predictions are merged with weighted box fusion, so a finding that both models agree on is counted once and ranked with higher confidence.

How to use it

  1. Create an account, then log in.
  2. Take a clear, well-lit photo of your teeth with a phone or camera.
  3. Upload the photo and wait a few seconds for the analysis.
  4. Review the highlighted regions and the number of possible caries found.
  5. If anything is flagged, or you have symptoms, book a visit with a dentist.

This tool supports, but does not replace, regular check-ups and professional advice.
